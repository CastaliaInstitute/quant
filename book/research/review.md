A useful reviewer is not asked whether the chart looks persuasive. They are asked to find the strongest reason the conclusion might be wrong.

## Review lenses

- **Statistics:** multiple testing, selection bias, clustered catalysts, non-stationarity, uncertainty intervals, and effective sample size.
- **Market microstructure:** press-release timestamps, after-hours reactions, spreads, volume capacity, auction mechanics, and realistic fills.
- **Biomedicine:** phase and endpoint comparability, control arms, indication heterogeneity, endpoint changes, missing/published results, and whether article context was knowable before the event.
- **Engineering:** data lineage, deterministic rebuilds, failure handling, and dependency versions.
- **Economics:** a plausible mechanism, competing explanations, and which participants might already arbitrage it away.
- **Ethics and stewardship:** who bears the downside, whether the presentation overstates certainty, and whether deployment is proportionate to evidence.

## Replication record

A publishable experiment should contain:

1. Hypothesis and falsification conditions written before final evaluation.
2. Immutable source identifiers and checksums for every dataset.
3. Exact instrument universe, calendar, timezone, and corporate-action handling.
4. Feature definitions and decision/execution timestamps.
5. Chronological train, validation, and untouched test ranges.
6. Model parameters, software versions, random seeds, and cost assumptions.
7. Results against baselines, uncertainty estimates, and sensitivity analysis.
8. Reviewer objections, revisions, and a clear evidence label.

## Provisional conclusion

The project now maps ten trial records to nine curated issuer disclosures across COVID-19 vaccination, Alzheimer’s disease, asthma, and oncology, with linked article metadata acquired for the registry cohort. The events include positive and negative readouts and explicit notes for paired-trial or joint-company disclosures. This remains a source-lineage fixture, not a fitted model: several records have date-only timestamps, the latest trial records can include information posted years after public disclosure, and licensed historical prices are not yet present. Before interpretation, archive point-in-time trial versions, resolve event-time precision, acquire appropriate prices, include inactive issuers, and preserve a final chronological holdout.
