THE CASTALIAN METHOD

Results are
earned, not assumed.

Our platform treats a backtest as a scientific claim. Every result must survive independent scrutiny.

01

Chronology is sacred

Train on the past. Validate on the future. Preserve a final holdout no model selection can touch.

02

Costs are part of the signal

Include turnover, transaction costs, and execution assumptions before calling an edge real.

03

Reproduce every result

Record the hypothesis, dataset version, parameters, windows, costs, and out-of-sample metrics.
